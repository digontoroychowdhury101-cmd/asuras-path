## Design prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: ONE definitive boss concept for Asura's Path — The Slumbering Titan, Ancient of the Sunken Crypt. COMPLETE REDESIGN from scratch. A frightening, physically powerful and unmistakable subterranean creature, not a noble stone dwarf or a generic rock golem.
Design thesis: an ancient predatory ORGANISM has grown through a ruined funerary idol, its petrified root sinews forcing the stone into an unnatural living anatomy. Suggest the uncomfortable weight, motion and intelligence of something buried alive for millennia. This is a living creature made of old rock and dead roots, not a man wearing armor. One coherent strong silhouette, believable weight and structural anatomy, deliberate asymmetry.
Anatomy and silhouette: colossal HUNCHED biped, tiny recessed head sunk between impossibly massive uneven shoulders, thick compact wedge-shaped torso and sloping back. One shoulder is a single enormous chipped slab with a heavy downward overhang; the other is lower, exposes bundled root tendons and a jagged shoulder blade. Huge long arms, one fist capable of crushing a person, oversized thick knuckles and short broad crushing stone fingers. Hands hang low but have clear space around the ankle strike zones. Powerful trunk-like upper thighs, lean exposed lower shins bound in dense taut woody tendons, wide heavy stone feet. The narrow lower legs must still feel load-bearing, like compressed twisted roots around dense stone cores. Bent knees, weight shifted forward, one foot advanced, intimidating stalking readiness, not a symmetrical standing character sheet pose. No bodybuilder six-pack, human breastplate, armor pauldrons, separate toy-like rock muscle plates or tiny ornamental details.
Face: a SMALL disturbing funerary face behind a brutally heavy fractured SLATE BROW/helm RGB(92,90,86), tilted subtly down toward the viewer. Long blank mask-like planes, chipped nose, deeply sunken mismatched eye slits with dim ember light far within, not friendly eyes, no big glowing eyeballs. Lower jaw seems partly missing beneath the face, an irregular darkness threaded with petrified ROOTS RGB(58,44,34). The root beard is a tangled mass of thick broken woody tendons fused into throat and sternum, not a groomed wizard beard or braided facial hair. One ancient branch antler rises crookedly back from a temple, the opposite branch antler is shorter, broken and splintered. Antlers are compact and sinister, not a decorative tree crown. Face is old, inhuman, hateful and unreadable; no handsome humanoid face, no smile, no regal dwarf king expression.
Materials: great weight-bearing slate masses with convincing large sedimentary breaks and worn smooth pressure surfaces. Organic dark petrified roots bridge the deep gaps at neck, armpits, elbows and hips like sinews. Irregular damp subterranean MOSS RGB(72,88,52) concentrated in sheltered crevices and on one side, not evenly scattered green speckles. A single brutal ancient corroded BRONZE GORGET RGB(120,92,56) is partly embedded into the root neck, worn almost featureless, no ornate fantasy jewelry or gold trim. A broken column haft is lodged diagonally against the back, partly enveloped by the root anatomy, no leather harness or clean metal buckles. Two ancient heavy bronze shackles remain around the exposed ANKLES, concentrated muted-hot AMBER seams RGB(255,170,60), distinct and reachable. Keep other light extremely restrained: a few deep dull ember faults under the sternum, NOT a bright lava chest, circular reactor, fire elemental, neon circuit diagram or full body of glowing cracks.
Strength and horror: protruding stone scapula, coarse uneven root flesh pulling under enormous loads, massive wrists, buried head and forward oppressive posture. Ancient violence conveyed by damage, shape and anatomy rather than dozens of spikes. Specific, memorable, grounded and sinister.
Art direction: hand-painted senior creature-design concept art with visible intentional gouache/oil brushwork, broad confident shape decisions, lost-and-found edges, restrained textural accents and strong charcoal value grouping. Organic imperfect drawing, selective sharp detail on face, hands and ankle shackles; quieter large stone planes elsewhere. A somber desaturated palette of cold slate, black-brown roots, moss and tarnished bronze, very limited amber accents. Hard oblique light and heavy natural occlusion carve the creature's mass. Do NOT use glossy 3D render style, synthetic procedural texture noise, uniform edge sharpness, airbrushed gradients, hyper-detailed ornament, excessive HDR bloom or generic AI fantasy splash-art polish.
Composition: single full-body creature in three-quarter view, clear silhouette, entire antlers, back-carried broken column, fingers and feet within frame with ample margin. No labels, typography, inset views, other phase, other characters or props outside the anatomy. Genuine TRANSPARENT alpha background. No arena, pedestal, floor, scenery, fog, rubble pile, floating rocks, magical aura or drop-shadow backdrop.
