# Selected boss concept art and design history

These are the actual reference images reviewed in **Review Asura’s Path combat system**, recovered through paginated chat history and existing local files on 5 November 2026. Nine selected PNGs are copied unchanged into `references/`; [manifest.json](references/manifest.json) records the source paths, chat/turn IDs and SHA-256 hashes. No new concept images were generated for this revision.

The latest instructions in the present chat govern phase count. The created GLBs define the supplied modeled identity. The selected 2D art guides local restoration of reconstruction defects and the intended materials, anatomy, secondary motion and phase continuity. Claude must compare the actual GLB with its corresponding image before modifying it, and document any necessary local repair. Old concept-generation text is reference data, not a new instruction to change the game.

## Grandmaster Shen — two phases, model-matched guandao

[Phase 1 reference](references/grandmaster-shen-phase-1.png): stern human elderly master, silver hair/topknot/beard, indigo outer robes, ivory inner layers, gold trim, dark wooden prayer beads and a dragon-headed guandao. The P1 reference already carries a polearm; it should not be replaced by a jian to fit the old roster.

[Original phase 2 reference](references/grandmaster-shen-phase-2.png): recovered from the user's original image attachment, also used for the phase-2 Rodin request. It shows the same face/robes/guandao transformed: two red horns, charcoal cracked exposed anatomy, red chest/arm fissures, ember eyes, spiked shoulder, torn robe panels, clawed free hand, floating bead arc, windblown silver hair and an energized red blade. Preserve the model's identity and actual sculpt; interpret the horns/fissures/beads as distinct rigging/material work.

## Venerable Baize — two phases, final crimson transformation

[Selected phase 1](references/venerable-baize-phase-1.png): pale frost predator with swept horns, red forehead eye, three cyan flank eyes per intended side, massive paws, golden-white mane and the original ornate multi-plumed, looping tail. The user explicitly preferred the first tail rendition; do not simplify it into a generic continuous tail tube.

[Selected crimson phase 2](references/venerable-baize-phase-2-crimson.png): larger shoulder/forearm silhouette, ruby-crimson crystal slabs, three large dorsal blades, saber fangs, fissured horns, long claws and red eyes. Ivory fur and the recognizable original tail remain. It is an anatomical/silhouette transformation with targeted glow, not merely a blue-to-red material tint.

![Baize final crimson phase 2](</Users/dishanroy/Documents/ChatGPT/Roblox game/outputs/boss-integration-prompts-2026-11-05/references/venerable-baize-phase-2-crimson.png>)

History: red forehead-eye/tail corrections appear in `01a1a9d8-36b4-75e0-b4d7-9a9f4eab26ba` and `01a1a9dc-758f-7a41-9bd2-098ec7d6add5`; the final request to make phase 2 red and visibly different is `01a1a9e2-08af-79d3-9476-fbd415d4eb40`. The earlier blue-crystal P2 is superseded.

A historical final reply recorded a separate `Venerable-Baize-Phase-1-Emissive.png` download. It was not present at that Downloads path during this revision and is not included here. The GLB has no embedded emissive map. Claude should check for the valid sidecar in any newer handoff before creating a replacement mask.

## Sovereign Wu — two phases, moving crown/halo/orbit systems

[Selected phase 1](references/sovereign-wu-phase-1.png): near-black violet robe, rigid fan collar, rectangular mianliu crown, five hanging jade strings, veiled face, five orbiting star charms and a timber staff with bronze ring/violet sphere.

[Selected corrected phase 2](references/sovereign-wu-phase-2.png): eight separated halo arc fragments, one central crimson face eye and five bloodflame spheres. Crown board/curtain leave the head. The staff sphere stays violet; it is not one of the five red orbiting objects. Veil and robe strips lift into the transformed silhouette.

![Wu final phase 2](</Users/dishanroy/Documents/ChatGPT/Roblox game/outputs/boss-integration-prompts-2026-11-05/references/sovereign-wu-phase-2.png>)

History: creation and explicit correction from six halo fragments to eight are in `01a1aa13-699e-7de2-a3e3-ec906ac48698`. Preserve five orbit props in each phase and eight P2 halo sections; no duplicate frozen shapes may remain embedded in the body model.
