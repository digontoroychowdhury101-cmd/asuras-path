# Animation coverage

Generated by `tools/animation/coverage.py` from the built catalog, the clip manifests and a Studio read-back of every id. It is not hand-maintained. **Published and loading does not mean it reads well:** the family sets are keyed from one parametric strike and pass the validators (reach, floor, sliding feet, weapon through body, pops). How they look in motion is human judgment, not yet done.

Read-back: 189 of 189 ids loaded with keyed poses (`mac-stage3/animation_readback.json`, Studio on the Mac); 0 not loaded; 0 empty.

## Bosses (BossAnimationRules, own resolver)

Bosses never use the sets above: `FighterAnimations` skips them and `BossAnimations` resolves `<Look>_P<n>_<Action>` from `AsuraBossAnimations` (General Zhao: 59 published clips in `assets/animations/general_zhao`). A boss's blade stays generic even when its schema weapon has a baked set (`CharacterService.commitStrike`). Slots without a clip fall back to a stock stand-in for humanoids and procedural motion for creatures, as listed by `tools/test_boss_presentation.luau`; `Catalog.Enemies` Shen slots: Shen_Phase1_Idle none, Shen_Phase1_SwordCombo none, Shen_Phase2_GlaiveCombo none, Shen_Phase2_Transform none. **Open:** boss contact alignment (pulses, signature attacks, scaled bodies) is unmeasured in Studio.
