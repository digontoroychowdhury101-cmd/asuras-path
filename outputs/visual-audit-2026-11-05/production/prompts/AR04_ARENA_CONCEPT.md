# AR04 — The Cathedral Beneath the Roots: environment concept prompt

Attach R02, R03, R09 from ../references. This optional concept is for the arena's layout/mood; upload isolated H-assets to Rodin separately.

```text
Create an original environment concept for Asura's Path, a monumental grim dark fantasy boss arena titled "The Cathedral Beneath the Roots". Use the attached reference images for scale, depth and material atmosphere rather than reproducing their exact landmarks, characters or HUD. A huge burial cathedral engulfed by a dead subterranean root system. The approach stays an intimate built crypt; the boss chamber opens into a cavern where pale petrified roots act as vast buttresses above fractured sepulchral architecture. A great split stone apse and a cold high fissure create layered depth behind the Titan. Graves/coffins and broken restraints gather at the margins; a shallow dark flooded ambulatory catches faint light outside the level central floor. Ancient limestone, mineral-white roots and umber crevices with sparse ember-bronze binding. The Titan remains the only active giant in the space, with its ankle weak points readable against calm stone. Descend through the existing gallery/ossuary scale, glimpse a root silhouette from the balcony, then pass under a heavy entrance arch into a chamber much larger than the approach suggested. Use a strong single overhead shaft and peripheral warm burial lights, not a low grid of straight ceiling beams. Show a default third-person player-height view from just inside the entrance, roughly 70-degree field of view. One small neutral player silhouette near the foreground and a simple non-designed mass at the intended boss station communicate scale; do not redesign or illustrate a new boss. Show a large navigable combat core clearly separated from peripheral terrain, believable building/landscape support, a visible entry and an implied return route. Three readable depth layers: tactile foreground frame, open midground fight, distant monumental landmark/skyline. Palette restrained and grounded, powerful silhouette hierarchy, asymmetry from age rather than random ornamental noise, calm walkable surfaces, natural wear at seams and foundations. No tiny diorama, fisheye lens, generic fantasy asset collage, overcrowded floor, excessive bloom, painted-on geometry, text or interface. Wide 16:9 composition. This is a level-design mood/scale image, not an image to upload as a whole 3D model to Rodin.
```

Then ask separately, using the approved image:

```text
Make a legible orthographic top-down layout study of this same approved arena. Preserve its actual circulation and landmark relationships. Distinguish flat combat ground, camera retreat band, entrance/exit/service pockets and nonwalkable scenery. Include a simple scale bar based on the chosen Roblox design dimensions, not dimensions guessed from the source screenshot. Do not add new obstacles, bosses or a second level. Return an image for visual planning; the agent must still implement and verify coordinates from the authored spatial manifest.
```
