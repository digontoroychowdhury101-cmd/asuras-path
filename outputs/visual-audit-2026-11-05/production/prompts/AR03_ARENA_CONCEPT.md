# AR03 — The Frost-Hollow Hunting Ground: environment concept prompt

Attach R07, R02, R05 from ../references. This optional concept is for the arena's layout/mood; upload isolated H-assets to Rodin separately.

```text
Create an original environment concept for Asura's Path, a monumental grim dark fantasy boss arena titled "The Frost-Hollow Hunting Ground". Use the attached reference images for scale, depth and material atmosphere rather than reproducing their exact landmarks, characters or HUD. An ancient hunting hollow beyond the cultivated bamboo shrine: low charcoal wet slate in the central fight clearing, pale frost-grass and snow caught at its edges, a few colossal wind-bent petrified roots, limestone ribs and deep distant bamboo. Four distinct ruined frost-perch pillars grow out of the terrain/court with meaningful silhouettes; their tops and approach paths remain mechanically readable. The white beast must contrast against darker central stone, while white reed fields and silver mist expand the horizon. Sparse red ritual cloth and dark bronze bells carry the sanctuary culture into a wilder older place. No uniform lawn border around a giant tiled rectangle. The visitor leaves a sheltered shrine path and passes under a ruined root/stone arch; near vegetation falls away to reveal an unexpectedly wide basin and distant frost ridges. Keep the moon-gate/ash descent exit recognizable without filling the combat clearing with stairs. Show a default third-person player-height view from just inside the entrance, roughly 70-degree field of view. One small neutral player silhouette near the foreground and a simple non-designed mass at the intended boss station communicate scale; do not redesign or illustrate a new boss. Show a large navigable combat core clearly separated from peripheral terrain, believable building/landscape support, a visible entry and an implied return route. Three readable depth layers: tactile foreground frame, open midground fight, distant monumental landmark/skyline. Palette restrained and grounded, powerful silhouette hierarchy, asymmetry from age rather than random ornamental noise, calm walkable surfaces, natural wear at seams and foundations. No tiny diorama, fisheye lens, generic fantasy asset collage, overcrowded floor, excessive bloom, painted-on geometry, text or interface. Wide 16:9 composition. This is a level-design mood/scale image, not an image to upload as a whole 3D model to Rodin.
```

Then ask separately, using the approved image:

```text
Make a legible orthographic top-down layout study of this same approved arena. Preserve its actual circulation and landmark relationships. Distinguish flat combat ground, camera retreat band, entrance/exit/service pockets and nonwalkable scenery. Include a simple scale bar based on the chosen Roblox design dimensions, not dimensions guessed from the source screenshot. Do not add new obstacles, bosses or a second level. Return an image for visual planning; the agent must still implement and verify coordinates from the authored spatial manifest.
```
