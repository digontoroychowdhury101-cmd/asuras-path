# AR05 — The Starless Mirror Court: environment concept prompt

Attach R04, R06, R03, R02 from ../references. This optional concept is for the arena's layout/mood; upload isolated H-assets to Rodin separately.

```text
Create an original environment concept for Asura's Path, a monumental grim dark fantasy boss arena titled "The Starless Mirror Court". Use the attached reference images for scale, depth and material atmosphere rather than reproducing their exact landmarks, characters or HUD. A drowned observatory court beneath a starless indigo sky. The playable surface is a level dark stone platform under a very shallow reflective-looking water layer; submerged radial inlays and broken stone seams establish footing. Far out, thin ruined arcades and a few impossibly tall dim vertical light/rain bands make the horizon feel immense. The Oracle throne remains an elevated lore focal point on the approach, and existing distant pagodas survive as remnants rather than every structure repeating the same roof. A broken astrolabe aperture frames the Oracle from entry. Blue-black water, desaturated violet stone, tarnished gold and restrained cold reflected light; phase-two bloodflame is a readable accent, not a permanent red room. The narrow eave-gallery/rafter traversal builds height and enclosure, then releases into a calm, vast-looking mirror court. Use the horizon and one celestial focal aperture to make the Oracle silhouette readable from the gate. Keep a visible return/dawn exit and a spatially believable link to the existing loft and bell. Show a default third-person player-height view from just inside the entrance, roughly 70-degree field of view. One small neutral player silhouette near the foreground and a simple non-designed mass at the intended boss station communicate scale; do not redesign or illustrate a new boss. Show a large navigable combat core clearly separated from peripheral terrain, believable building/landscape support, a visible entry and an implied return route. Three readable depth layers: tactile foreground frame, open midground fight, distant monumental landmark/skyline. Palette restrained and grounded, powerful silhouette hierarchy, asymmetry from age rather than random ornamental noise, calm walkable surfaces, natural wear at seams and foundations. No tiny diorama, fisheye lens, generic fantasy asset collage, overcrowded floor, excessive bloom, painted-on geometry, text or interface. Wide 16:9 composition. This is a level-design mood/scale image, not an image to upload as a whole 3D model to Rodin.
```

Then ask separately, using the approved image:

```text
Make a legible orthographic top-down layout study of this same approved arena. Preserve its actual circulation and landmark relationships. Distinguish flat combat ground, camera retreat band, entrance/exit/service pockets and nonwalkable scenery. Include a simple scale bar based on the chosen Roblox design dimensions, not dimensions guessed from the source screenshot. Do not add new obstacles, bosses or a second level. Return an image for visual planning; the agent must still implement and verify coordinates from the authored spatial manifest.
```
