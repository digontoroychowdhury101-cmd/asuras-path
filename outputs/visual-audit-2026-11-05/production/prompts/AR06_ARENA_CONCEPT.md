# AR06 — The Storm-Crowned Expanse: environment concept prompt

Attach R05, R08, R01, R03 from ../references. This optional concept is for layout/mood.

Check for an existing approved scene concept in the project and ForgeGUI library first. If a new concept is useful and within this batch, use a verified ForgeGUI opaque widescreen image route (currently generation_image type thumbnail) or the creator's chosen image tool. Use the following text as the visual brief. A scene image guides composition; create only missing individual 3D props through ForgeGUI and construct precise playable space with Blender/Roblox. See [ASSET_WORKFLOW.md](../ASSET_WORKFLOW.md).

```text
Create an original environment concept for Asura's Path, a monumental grim dark fantasy boss arena titled "The Storm-Crowned Expanse". Use the attached reference images for scale, depth and material atmosphere rather than reproducing their exact landmarks, characters or HUD. A wind-scoured summit of an ancient ruined city, with a vast dark slate landing shelf and storm-pale ash/frost at the outer margins. Broken domed rotundas and aqueduct ribs ride distant isolated cliffs. A tall storm-split beacon/perch anchors the far silhouette and holds a few believable broken chain runs. The supplied charcoal wyrm should be legible against cold low cloud and subdued pale ground, not blue-on-blue flatness. Huge sky, sparse surviving architecture and deep ravines create scale; central air and landing corridors stay open. Weathered neutral stone, black basalt, mineral-white damage and restrained cold storm light, with no default saturated azure castle. The repaired narrow saddle bridge and Heaven Stair hold the view to a tall distant silhouette. Emerging through the bastion reveals the enormous landing expanse and an unbroken sky line above the Wyrm. Keep the return-to-Shrine destination readable and sheltered at the bastion, not inside the active flight floor. Show a default third-person player-height view from just inside the entrance, roughly 70-degree field of view. One small neutral player silhouette near the foreground and a simple non-designed mass at the intended boss station communicate scale; do not redesign or illustrate a new boss. Show a large navigable combat core clearly separated from peripheral terrain, believable building/landscape support, a visible entry and an implied return route. Three readable depth layers: tactile foreground frame, open midground fight, distant monumental landmark/skyline. Palette restrained and grounded, powerful silhouette hierarchy, asymmetry from age rather than random ornamental noise, calm walkable surfaces, natural wear at seams and foundations. No tiny diorama, fisheye lens, generic fantasy asset collage, overcrowded floor, excessive bloom, painted-on geometry, text or interface. Wide 16:9 composition. This is a level-design mood/scale image, not a whole-arena 3D generation request.
```

Then ask separately, using the approved image:

```text
Make a legible orthographic top-down layout study of this same approved arena. Preserve its actual circulation and landmark relationships. Distinguish flat combat ground, camera retreat band, entrance/exit/service pockets and nonwalkable scenery. Include a simple scale bar based on the chosen Roblox design dimensions, not dimensions guessed from the source screenshot. Do not add new obstacles, bosses or a second level. Return an image for visual planning; the agent must still implement and verify coordinates from the authored spatial manifest.
```
