# AR01 — The Ashen Covenant: environment concept prompt

Attach R01, R03, R07, R09 from ../references. This optional concept is for the arena's layout/mood; upload isolated H-assets to Rodin separately.

```text
Create an original environment concept for Asura's Path, a monumental grim dark fantasy boss arena titled "The Ashen Covenant". Use the attached reference images for scale, depth and material atmosphere rather than reproducing their exact landmarks, characters or HUD. An immense roofless funerary amphitheatre, not a bright miniature training court. A broad ash-pale fighting floor sits within worn radial seating fronts and shattered arcades. One split black cenotaph with an austere empty vertical cleft dominates the far horizon, backed by pale dust-lit cloud and distant ruined bridges; it is original architecture, not a copy of a recognizable tree/gate from the references. Sparse silver reeds, buried weapon fragments and worn votive figures occupy the outer ash banks. Charcoal/basalt/old bronze against muted cold-grey ash, with tiny ember warmth and no blanket orange grading. Approach through a low, dark champion arcade, then step out into the full sky and long view of the split monument; the boss is the first moving silhouette against a calmer floor. The Victor exit remains legible in the far-side composition after victory. Show a default third-person player-height view from just inside the entrance, roughly 70-degree field of view. One small neutral player silhouette near the foreground and a simple non-designed mass at the intended boss station communicate scale; do not redesign or illustrate a new boss. Show a large navigable combat core clearly separated from peripheral terrain, believable building/landscape support, a visible entry and an implied return route. Three readable depth layers: tactile foreground frame, open midground fight, distant monumental landmark/skyline. Palette restrained and grounded, powerful silhouette hierarchy, asymmetry from age rather than random ornamental noise, calm walkable surfaces, natural wear at seams and foundations. No tiny diorama, fisheye lens, generic fantasy asset collage, overcrowded floor, excessive bloom, painted-on geometry, text or interface. Wide 16:9 composition. This is a level-design mood/scale image, not an image to upload as a whole 3D model to Rodin.
```

Then ask separately, using the approved image:

```text
Make a legible orthographic top-down layout study of this same approved arena. Preserve its actual circulation and landmark relationships. Distinguish flat combat ground, camera retreat band, entrance/exit/service pockets and nonwalkable scenery. Include a simple scale bar based on the chosen Roblox design dimensions, not dimensions guessed from the source screenshot. Do not add new obstacles, bosses or a second level. Return an image for visual planning; the agent must still implement and verify coordinates from the authored spatial manifest.
```
