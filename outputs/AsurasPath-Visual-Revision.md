# Shrine visual revision — 18 October 2026

The opening area has been rebuilt as an overcast Wuxia temple courtyard. This is a revision to the playable starting shrine; the other story regions have not received this art pass.

## Changes

- Replaced the open disc composition with roofed corner cloisters, laid flagstones, paired reflecting basins, memorial tablets, and an axial approach to the gateway.
- Rebuilt the gateway as a two-tier paifang gatehouse with layered tile eaves and jade mist. Kept the existing Monastery destination and portal interaction.
- Replaced the oversized bonfire with a low bronze ritual bowl, incense sticks, restrained flame, and smoke. Updated the checkpoint height and spawn placement.
- Replaced plastic cloud slabs and spheres with soft mist banks and angular karst mountain silhouettes.
- Changed the lighting to a cool, desaturated overcast palette with localized warm lanterns. Reduced bloom and sun rays.
- Reduced the health/stamina and loadout HUD to 65% of its previous size, moved vitals clear of the Roblox menu, reduced announcement text, and scaled down the altar panel.
- Added a local-geometry wanderer outfit, woven travelling hat, face wrap, sash, robe panels, and bracers. Wait for the character limbs and reapply appearance/weapon visuals after Roblox finishes loading the avatar.
- Reduced repeated roof seams and railing pieces following the first Studio review.

## Validation

All six existing automated suites passed after the final changes, and all 35 active Luau sources compiled. The saved place maps all 35 sources.

An initial courtyard revision was launched in Studio and visually inspected. That inspection exposed overly dark lighting and missing outfit pieces after avatar assembly; both were revised in source. The initial courtyard booted both server and client successfully.
