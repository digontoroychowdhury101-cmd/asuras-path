## What the inspection showed
The existing pale veiled ascetic and enormous twisted tree already supply the lobby's identity. The current shrine has a central bonfire, two rectangular basins, a narrow staircase/gateway, side pavilions and a much larger upper terrace. The captured player view shows dark paving, opaque-looking pools, basic eaves and railings, and large sparse columns around the statue. These make the surrounding construction feel unfinished beside the hero asset.

## Proposed direction
Preserve the existing statue/tree composition and its main approach. Give the surrounding space a coherent Chinese wuxia architectural language: charcoal tiled roofs, dark vermilion timber, carved grey stone, small bronze details and amber lanterns. Use sparse crimson leaves to connect the courtyard to the sacred tree.

Make the lower courtyard a calm, readable gathering place. Widen the main route visually, keep the rest altar low, improve water clarity and reflection, and introduce varied stone joints, edge wear and restrained moss. Build connected cloisters into the side destinations so each has an obvious entrance and purpose.

Shape the upper terrace as layered rock and masonry. Concentrate dressing near landings and the statue's seat; preserve broad open space and camera sightlines. Replace repetitive plain columns with a smaller set of distinctive carved stelae or ruined gallery fragments.

Light for play: readable neutral fill across floors and faces, warm low-angle sunlight, localized lantern pools, restrained jade at travel points. Keep the statue pale against the tree and sky.

## Translation priorities
1. Set lighting, stone values and water before adding small props.
2. Replace roofs, rails, stairs and terrace edges with a consistent reusable kit.
3. Give the forge and duel court clear connected approaches.
4. Dress the upper terrace while preserving the imported statue/tree.
5. Add sparse foliage, cloth, workshop props and distant landscape silhouettes.

## Interpretation limits
These are aspirational illustrations, not measured plans or implemented Roblox screenshots. The elevated image shifts the descent pavilion to a side branch for visibility; the existing source places it behind the south arrival. Its exact placement and the reliquary jumps need a layout pass before implementation. The images also crop the enormous tree canopy and add distant roofs/waterfalls for atmosphere; those are optional and do not imply new required regions. Preserve the actual imported asset proportions and verify all routes, jump gaps, player camera views and performance in Studio when building.
