Continue beyond six hours when required for reachable-content coverage; mark inaccessible content precisely.

Log pauses separately. Every thirty active minutes report elapsed play, locations reached, enemy/boss coverage and mechanics tested. Setup or source-audit work does not advance the clock.

## One-to-one observation schema

Each numbered entry needs active elapsed HH:MM, wall timestamp, location/direction, player level/loadout, action, result, screenshot/clip reference, and confidence (`observed`, `measured`, `estimated`, `publisher claim`, `unverified`). Tie every proposed Asura gap to a reference note plus the actual existing source consumer. Keep original Asura design decisions distinct from reference behavior.

Region entries: entry/exit/return edges; landmarks and sightlines; route length/time; height bands; walkable slopes; materials and transitions; tree/rock/prop density; water behavior; buildings and interior layout; safe/combat zones; NPCs, loot and secrets. Estimate sizes only with a stated scale calibration and error range.

Environment entries: visual/audio changes and duration; observed sky/weather states; indoor/outdoor transition; light/colour estimates labeled estimates; visibility during attacks. An unobserved weather state remains unknown, not absent. A screenshot cannot reveal exact Atmosphere or Lighting values.

Enemy/boss entries: visible name/model, spawn and return route, attack tell/response, attempt outcome, health/damage evidence, phase transition, measured cooldown samples, rewards and retry/reset. Separate unknown internal health from visible health-bar fractions. Drop frequency needs numerator/denominator; no inferred exact drop rate from one kill.

System entries: controls and UI path, resource costs, unlocks, formula observations with samples, death losses, shop prices, equipment changes, dialogue/quest state, saving/reconnect behavior. Do not spend Robux, erase data or change account settings to complete the survey.
