## Finish the art before importing

1. **Tree first.** Compare the silhouette at the reference camera, another front oblique, both sides and above the lower crown. Sculpt two or three unmistakable asymmetrical leaders with clearly separated planes and branches that follow their growing direction. Use the fused bark as the high sculpt. At the main forks form a broad, rounded branch collar into the parent and carry grooved grain through the transition. Thin secondary and tertiary limbs early. Check adjacent twig starts in a neutral clay view or with section cuts so seams are visible. In tight branch neighborhoods remove redundant forks and use a few longer arcs to carve readable negative space. Preserve asymmetry and the bare crown.
2. **Make the roots grip the temple.** Inspect the v10 side where the bright vertical bands hang like cable bundles. Reduce parallel sequences, vary thickness along the fall and create shorter branching spans with visible contact at the wall, gallery and stone ledge. Anchor key roots to several points along stone faces. Leave gaps between major ropes and submerge tips behind blocks or into cracks. Integrate dark crevices, pale upper grain and gradual moss at genuine contacts. Favor the creator reference’s strong left-hand sweep from the hero camera while dressing the other sides. Check stair head and overhead clearance from player eye height.
3. **Finish both guardians as characters.** First inspect actual placement, mirrored winding, normal shading and base contact in saved v10. Use the scan as a base and sculpt a more compact protective stance, prominent brows, clenched muzzle, large paws and deliberately curled mane appropriate to the reference. Treat the visible escutcheon as a replaceable motif. Fit an original weathered wheel or lotus motif, or cover it with a carved architectural element that fits the composition. Vary the two poses, scars, moss and lip wear. Slight yaw and a mirrored mesh are only a starting point. Add more vertical shape and joint articulation to the plinths, vary stone course erosion and make the feet meet the base. Review clean silhouettes from the lower stair, along the rail, from the front at avatar scale and from their backs. Credit the source scan and modifications in `assets/CREDITS.md` and `outputs/vesperhold/THIRD_PARTY.md` when the scan ships.
4. **Replace visible box masses with designed foundations.** From both sides and especially the v10 rear view, rebuild the block stacks as stepped masonry sitting on irregular bedrock. Put visible piers or buttresses underneath elevated structures, introduce soil and broken stone at the base, and break long planar walls into deep offsets, individual weathered courses and scoured ledges. Dress upper backs and side galleries with selective true walkways, bracing, closed shutters and recesses. Keep modular architecture legible behind the roots. Tone down fog for check renders so you catch floating details and unconvincing supports. Then restore deep cool mist, localized warm amber and damp material variation. Fog should frame the forms and leave key edges readable.
5. **Finish the player experience.** Keep the long broad stair, a small avatar or traveler for scale, both lions, six architectural tiers and the giant crown visible in the hero portrait. In additional player-height views inspect the climb, gate threshold, gallery silhouettes and water approach. In closeups, prioritize chipped stairs and roof tiles, aged red lacquer, gold wheel reliefs, changing bracket patterns, broken stone and selective moss. Keep the player’s centerline clear. Coordinate banners, gong, vines, broken colonnade, wrecked boat and lake vegetation with the actual world layout.
6. **Approve through evidence.** Render a portrait beside the exact supplied image, a player-view approach and elevation views all around, plus closeups of each guardian, two structural forks and at least one upper and lower root contact. Compare in both neutral light and storm lighting. Document remaining defects. The stop occurred before the v10 guardian pair and tree closeups were judged in context.

## Replace the actual temple in Roblox

1. **Prove the final world fit.** Keep the branch’s enlarged basin and the existing gate-to-rim-to-forest route. Use `LandmarkLayout.luau`, `LandmarkWorld.luau` and `work/sol-bellwood-v9/world_fit.luau` to check final mesh bounds against the tunnel exit, lake, gate, approach and neighboring road. In the existing v9 probe, all eight surveyed road eyes saw the upper roof and crown through a terrain-only test. The threshold and old hall were hidden from all eight. Test a nearer natural rock screen and a lower or deeper temple foundation while keeping the scale. Check actual camera behavior with terrain present before settling the new placement. In earlier Studio runs the distant rim was absent in the camera even when client and server agreed on terrain occupancy. Extra replication focus and temporary persistent rock segments did not recover it.
2. **Author production meshes and texture maps.** Keep the Blender study as the high source. Use intentional low topology from the procedural bark and architecture, with more resolution at hero forks, roots, faces and eaves. Author twig density and cross-section sampling for the level of detail they need. Give stairs, gate and interior walking surfaces independent collision geometry. Separate roots, roofs, props, vegetation and mist for rendering and stream-friendly placement. Keep upload submeshes around 17.5k triangles and under the project’s 18–20k cap, keep normal and UV continuity along partition lines, and bake 2048 maps. Do not use a Decimate modifier or treat the project as having a total triangle cap. Reuse `tools/blender/vesperhold/vesp_bake.py` and the existing temple-hall pipeline. `split_cap` partitions existing triangles to enforce upload limits. It cannot reduce the millions of source triangles. A direct cut of the study would leave too many pieces and a costly load.
3. **Bake, pilot and verify registry readback.** Follow Windows handoff §5 for the raw branch, 2048 material bakes, `vesp_embed`, `glb_split.py` and upload ledger. Keep parts ≤20,000,000 bytes. Upload the smallest representative piece first to the Development asset scope, read back the actual model and surface roles, then proceed only if the asset passes. Merge the previous ledger before readback because registry generation rewrites the landmark registry. Register the candidate pack with a distinct name such as `bellwood_temple_colossal`, add the manifest fixture and regenerate the Luau registry. Use the actual chosen origin and read-back mapping from Blender (x, y, z) to Roblox model (−x, z, y). Do not guess IDs or overwrite the stable old hall’s source registry entry while evaluating the candidate.
4. **Implement a real candidate replacement and test it.** Use the existing `Layout.Basin.Temple` and `LandmarkWorld.templeFrame` path. Recalculate the foot frame, stairs, approach/causeway connection and position-dependent accents and sound emitters. Keep decorative roles noncollidable under the existing Fidelity map. Assert layout points, collision roles and availability fallback where appropriate in the existing tests.
