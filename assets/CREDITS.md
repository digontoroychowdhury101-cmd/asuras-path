## Shrine lobby rebuild — 2026-10-21/22

The Shrine of the Ascendant's modular architecture kit (roofs, timber frame, brackets, balustrades,
lanterns, stelae, ruins, basin coping, bonfire dais, cliffs, karst towers), its cloud, maple, shrub and
banner pieces, all eleven tileable PBR texture sets and the original six procedural golden-hour sky faces were generated for
this project by `tools/blender/lobby_kit.py`, `lobby_kit_*.py`, `lobby_textures.py` and
`tools/assets/make_sunset_sky.py`. No third-party models, scans or textures were downloaded or used.
That sky describes the first rebuild iteration; the current sky and new branch cutout are recorded below.

## Sanctuary vista landscape kit — 2026-10-22

The Crown, Ridge, Needle and Waterfall geometry in `AsuraSanctuaryVista` was authored for this project
with `tools/blender/sanctuary_vista.py`. Its rock color, normal and roughness maps reuse the existing
Poly Haven [Rock Face 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03) CC0 source already recorded above,
resized to 1024 for this kit. Moss and water use plain authored materials; the optional black metalness
map comes from the existing generated lobby kit. No new external assets were downloaded.

## Sanctuary sky, foliage and guardian — 2026-10-22/23

The current golden-hour panorama and transparent crimson maple-branch spray were generated as original
project assets with the built-in imagegen tool on 22 October 2026. No reference-image inputs were
supplied. Complete generation prompts are preserved in `assets/build/sanctuary_sky/prompt.md` and
`crimson_branch_prompt.md`; original outputs are `panorama.png` and `crimson_branch.png` in that folder.
`tools/assets/panorama_to_sanctuary_sky.py` projects the panorama into six cube faces, including corrected
cap orientation. Actual uploaded image IDs and the maple texture are in `assets/SANCTUARY_SKY_UPLOADED.json`.
The older leaf-particle/scatter masks continue to use the ambientCG Leaf Set 024 credit above.

The guardian lion was generated through the installed `tools/trellis/run_trellis.sh` on 23 October
2026: Pollinations produced the reference image and microsoft/TRELLIS.2 reconstructed the sculpture.
Conservative Blender cleanup flattened/closed the lower plinth and retained the generated PBR maps.
The cleaned asset contains 17,622 triangles with 1024 Color/Normal/Roughness/Metalness maps. The raw
generation, editable `.blend`, cleanup script and validation are preserved under
`assets/build/guardian_lion/`; the generation reference is under `work/trellis/Asuraguardianlion/`.
`assets/GUARDIAN_LION_UPLOADED.json` records the imported mesh/map IDs, hash and eight-stud template.
This is generated-asset provenance; no third-party lion mesh was downloaded. The earlier hero sculpt's
generation-service gap noted above remains unresolved.

## Sanctuary detail kit — 23 October 2026

`assets/build/sanctuary_detail/AsuraSanctuaryDetail.blend` and `.glb` are original Blender-authored modular erosion, fern and crimson maple meshes generated by `tools/blender/sanctuary_detail.py`. Rock SurfaceAppearances reuse the already credited Poly Haven `rock_face_03` CC0 maps and uploaded Vista material; foliage uses original mesh geometry and the existing generated crimson branch alpha image. | Effekseer speed easing — Effekseer Project | [Original implementation](https://github.com/effekseer/Effekseer/blob/8b1e2969825638fb24682f3697610d8f37949d22/Dev/Editor/EffekseerCore/Utils/Math.cs) | MIT | Luau adaptation of MathUtl.Easing for combat ember envelopes. |
| Effekseer sample textures — Effekseer Project / AndrewFM | [Sample pack](https://github.com/effekseer/Effekseer/tree/8b1e2969825638fb24682f3697610d8f37949d22/Release/Sample/01_AndrewFM01) | CC0-1.0 | Parry wind and wave strip, Blazing Sun flame and smoke; spark and thunder masks registered as reserves. |

Texture uploads returned real Roblox IDs and all six passed a typed Studio image preload.
`assets/GITHUB_UPLOADED.json` records them. Five texture variants are used in the game; spark and vertical thunder masks
are registered for future effects. The 90-degree lightning variant has now passed typed Studio preload and is used by Celestial line spells.
The two physical impacts are uploaded, typed-preloaded and active on unarmed/heavy contacts and shockwaves:

Six sample textures by AndrewFM / the Effekseer contributors, from `Release/Sample/01_AndrewFM01/Texture/`, have an explicit CC0 dedication in `assets/github/effekseer/CC0-SAMPLES.txt`; this is separate from the editor's MIT license. The prepared masks convert black-background additive textures into white RGBA, retain existing alpha, and cap either dimension at 1024.
- `impact_big.wav`: FFeller (2020), CC0-1.0, via [Godot's per-file record](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects/blob/b761b4cd5718a7118f2c95eb55f181d0a5f004f1/3d/ragdoll_physics/sounds/impact_big.LICENSE.md), originally [Freesound 532873](https://freesound.org/people/FFeller/sounds/532873/).
- `impact_small.wav`: dorian.mastin (2017), CC0-1.0, via [Godot's per-file record](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-demo-projects/blob/b761b4cd5718a7118f2c95eb55f181d0a5f004f1/3d/ragdoll_physics/sounds/impact_small.LICENSE.md), originally [Freesound 381626](https://freesound.org/people/dorian.mastin/sounds/381626/).

Godot's copies have leading silence trimmed upstream. The local preparation resamples them to
48 kHz mono PCM16, removes DC, applies 5 ms end fades and normalizes to a -6 dBFS peak.
Originals, prepared files, Git/LFS identities and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
`assets/GITHUB_REUSE.json`. No sound or image IDs were invented; existing owned image/audio slots
remain available alongside the new GitHub texture slots.
